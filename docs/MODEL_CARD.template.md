## Intended use
Research and education: studying whether optical/SWIR Sentinel-2 patches carry a recognisable signature of FIRMS-style active-fire detections.

## Out of scope
**Not for emergency response, evacuation, dispatch, early warning, insurance, or any safety-critical decision.** The model cannot see through cloud or smoke, only sees daylight imagery, and is not validated against ground truth.

## Known failure modes
Review `reports/false_positives.png` and `reports/false_negatives.png` and write what you actually see here. Expected candidates: industrial heat and solar farms, bright bare soil and burn scars, cloud edges, small fires, smoke or cloud over the fire, FIRMS/Sentinel-2 time mismatch, registration error.

## Ethical considerations
False negatives could give false reassurance; false positives could cause alarm. Fire detections can overlap with sensitive locations (homes, infrastructure). Do not present outputs as authoritative.

## Caveats
Single region and season unless you extend it; no calibration guarantee outside the test distribution.
